Oracle to Postgres. Zero downtime. 60% cheaper.
We did it for an enterprise.
The trap is PL/SQL. A naive move breaks your app logic.
So we rewrote 150 plus procedures into PL/pgSQL, with tests.
Then AWS DMS kept both databases in sync.
Cutover took zero minutes of downtime.
Top 100 queries ran 3.5 times faster.
It paid for itself in 6 months.
