# Voice: CodesLytic (codeslytic.com)
Source: brand-kit/docs/brand-guidelines.md, llms-full.txt. No past reels exist; refine after first posts.

- Speaker: engineer talking to a buyer, over screen recording. No face on camera.
- Tone: precise, calm, confident, practical. Never hype.
- Lead with the client's problem, not the tech stack.
- Numbers and named technologies over adjectives.
- Short sentences, active voice, contractions.
- Never say: synergy, cutting-edge, revolutionary, game-changer, unlock, leverage, "in today's fast-paced world".
- Never name a client without confirmed permission. Say "a manufacturer", "an enterprise".
- Audience: founders, ops heads, CTOs in USA, UAE, India needing ERP, micro ERP, custom SaaS, websites, apps, data work.
- End card: logo + codeslytic.com. Hashtags max 3-5. CTA: one only.
- Visuals: dark #0C0805, orange #FEA849, blue #6DADFF, Sora headings, Inter body, IBM Plex Mono labels.
